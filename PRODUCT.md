# ImageStudio

## Register

product

## Users

Timo and creators generating, comparing and exporting images, AgentZ thumbnails, logos, print layouts and short video assets on a desktop.

## Product Purpose

Bring creation, references and reusable projects into one local studio, equally operable by a person and through its authenticated local MCP interface.

## Brand Personality

Focused, confident, clear. Option A from the September 2026 HTML design study was explicitly selected by the user.

## Anti-references

Stacked horizontal switchers, model-chip navigation, decorative purple glows, tiny low-contrast text, and chat-shaped image editing. Higgsfield's media-first dark creation surface is the reference; ImageStudio uses its own stable sidebar and local workflow.

## Design Principles

- Pictures carry the visual color; interface color indicates action and selection.
- Navigation, organization and generation have stable and separate places.
- Progressive disclosure retains supported options without making every control primary.
- Existing work survives navigation and feature retirement.
- UI and MCP share domain behavior, live state and persistence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable controls, WCAG AA contrast targets, visible keyboard focus, labelled actions, reduced motion, and usable narrow desktop windows.
